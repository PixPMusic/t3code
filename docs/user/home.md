# Home

Home is an optional agent that helps you manage work across your projects and linked environments. Ask it what is running, have it start a thread in a project, or ask it to follow work and report back. Messages it sends to other threads are attributed to Home.

Open **Settings → Home**, choose a model, and turn Home on for that environment. Open it from navigation, the command palette, or **⌘⇧0** (**Ctrl⇧0** on Windows/Linux). On mobile, Home opens as a full-screen conversation, including on tablets and foldables.

Home uses the same providers, permissions, and linked environment access as other agents. Link another machine in **Settings → Environments** before asking Home to work there. A link with narrower access still limits Home; full access is needed to answer permission requests. Home can answer questions and approvals when you ask it to, but cannot delete projects or threads.

Threads Home launches are watched automatically. You can also ask it to watch another thread, stop watching one, or watch all work. Home reports completions, failures, questions, and approvals without a desktop window staying open. Reports resume after the server restarts or an established link reconnects. When watching all work, a newly linked machine or a machine that was unreachable when watching began starts being observed once Home can reach it.

**Start fresh** gives Home a new conversation with the same model. Files, notes, and your additions to Home's instructions remain in its managed folder. The previous Home stops and loses access to T3's tools. Turning Home off also stops it and removes its watches; its conversation remains in history.
